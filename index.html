Jang Family Watch AI

Jang Family Watch AI is a private Apple Watch app for members of one family. It is a chat client for an AI assistant that runs on the family's own home server. It is not a public service: you can only use it with an account created for you by the family.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

What the app sends

  • Your username and password, to sign in to the family's server.
  • The messages you type or dictate, so the assistant can answer them.

This information is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the family's own server, which is run by a parent at home.

AI processing and web search

Answers for this app are written by an AI model that runs on the family's own computer. Your messages are not sent to a hosted AI model provider. When an answer needs current information, the search question may be sent to a web search provider (Tavily). Your username and password are never sent to that provider. Its handling of the search text is governed by its own terms: Tavily Privacy Policy.

What is stored

  • On the watch: your username and password are saved in the device Keychain so you only sign in once. Signing out in the app deletes them. Chat messages are not saved on the watch; they disappear when the app closes.
  • On the family's server: messages and replies are kept in server logs to keep the assistant working and to fix problems. For family members, the assistant may also remember facts from conversations (for example, a preference you mentioned) so that later answers are more helpful. Guest and review accounts are not remembered.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • No access to your location, contacts, health data, or photos.
  • No selling of data, and no sharing with other companies beyond the web search described above.

Children

The app is used by children in the family under their parents' supervision. Accounts are created only by a parent. For children's accounts, each conversation is checked on the family's own computer, and a parent may be emailed an exchange that raises a safety concern. A parent can review or delete a child's account and server data at any time.

Deleting your data

Sign out in the app to remove the saved sign-in from the watch. To delete an account or server data, ask the family administrator using the contact below.
