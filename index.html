Jang Family Watch AI

This Apple Watch app shows one Discord channel chosen by a parent, and lets the person wearing the watch read it and send messages to it. It works on watches set up with Apple Watch For Your Kids (Family Setup), without an iPhone. A parent connects the watch on the setup page using a Discord bot of their own.

The app is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Discord Inc.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The app's maker receives no data

The app has no server of its own. Messages, account details and setup details are never sent to the app's maker. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking, and no data is sold.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • To Discord: the messages you type or dictate, which are posted to the channel the parent chose, and requests to read that channel's recent messages. Everyone who can see that channel can read them. Discord handles this data under its own Privacy Policy.
  • To Apple's iCloud (pairing only): the watch publishes a one-time public key, and the setup page sends back the bot token, channel ID and display name, encrypted so only that watch can read them. Both records are deleted once pairing finishes, and unused ones stop working after 10 minutes. The parent signs in with their Apple Account on the setup page to send them.

Before anything is sent, the app shows a screen that explains this and asks you to agree. Nothing is sent until you tap Agree.

What is stored

  • On the watch: the bot token, channel and display name are kept in the watch's Keychain, which stays on that device. Unpairing deletes them and removes the app's webhook from the channel. Messages are not saved on the watch.
  • In Discord: messages sent from the watch stay in the channel like any other Discord message, until someone with permission deletes them.

What the app does not access

No location, contacts, health data, photos, or other Discord channels or servers.

Children

The app is meant for families: a parent sets it up with their own Discord bot and chooses the one channel the watch can see, and the parent controls who else is in that channel. Discord requires its own users to be at least 13; the person wearing the watch does not sign in to Discord and does not need a Discord account. Parents can unpair the watch, delete the bot, or delete messages in Discord at any time.

Deleting your data

Unpair in the app's settings to remove the stored details from the watch. Delete messages in Discord. Deleting the bot in the Discord Developer Portal stops all access at once.
