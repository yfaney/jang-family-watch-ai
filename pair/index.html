Set up a watch

Connect a watch to one channel in your Discord server. The watch shows a pairing code; enter it here with your bot's details. The details are encrypted on this page so only that watch can read them.

1. Prepare a Discord bot (once)

  1. In the Discord Developer Portal, create an application, open Bot, and copy its token.
  2. On the same page, turn on Message Content Intent.
  3. Paste the token below, then use the invite link that appears to add the bot to your server. It asks for View Channel, Read Message History and Manage Webhooks.
  4. In Discord, turn on Developer Mode (Settings › Advanced), right-click the channel and choose Copy Channel ID.

2. Sign in with your Apple Account

The details are delivered through Apple's iCloud, which needs you to be signed in. Nothing is stored in your iCloud Drive.

3. Enter the details

Your bot token goes only to the watch, encrypted. It is never sent to the app's maker. Privacy Policy
